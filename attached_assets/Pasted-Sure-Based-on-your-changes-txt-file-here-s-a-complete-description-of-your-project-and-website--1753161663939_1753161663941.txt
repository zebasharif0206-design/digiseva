## 🧾 **Project Overview**

**Title (suggested)**: *Service Directory – A Home Services Web Platform*

## 🌐 **What is the Website About?**

It's a **Streamlit-based web application** where users can sign up to request home services (like cleaning, plumbing, electrical, etc.), and professionals can sign up to offer those services. An admin panel also exists to manage the system.

---

## 👥 **Who are the Users?**

1. **Customers (Users)** – to book services
2. **Professionals** – to offer services
3. **Admin** – to oversee everything

Each user type sees a different **dashboard** after login.
